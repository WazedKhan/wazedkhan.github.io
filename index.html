Bugs I've squashed

Three stories from production. Nobody throws a party when the bug doesn't happen, so I'm telling you instead.

The 30-second API

HealthOS pages hung every busy afternoon. The cause was N+1 queries hiding in serializers, plus hot lookups with no index. I profiled each endpoint, redesigned the indexes and cached the reads that barely changed in Redis.

Responses went from about 30s to 2s, a 93% drop.

Billed twice for one order

Under load, two requests could both check "is there an invoice yet?" before either one saved it. I wrapped invoice creation in a transaction, locked the row with select_for_update(), and added a unique constraint as the last line of defence.

No more duplicate invoices for about 4,500 users.

The day the lead left

The tech lead resigned and nobody else really knew the backend, while about 5,000 people used it every day. I took over production support, kept it stable, then stepped up as acting tech lead for a team of seven.

It stayed up, and I learned to lead.
